## Context

## Decision

## Alternatives considered

Briefly explain meaningful alternatives and why they were not chosen. Omit this section when there was no meaningful alternative.

## Consequences

## References

Link the relevant product reference, milestone plan, previous ADRs and delivery evidence when available. Do not imply that a planned capability has already shipped.
