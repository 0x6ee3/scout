# Scout — Feature wishlist

A collection of rough product ideas and brainstorming notes. These are **not planned milestones, approved requirements or commitments for v1 or v2**. Order does not indicate priority. Add ideas freely; refine and prioritize only when actual use demonstrates value.

The former roadmap's “Milestone 7 — Future improvements” has moved here. The [portfolio roadmap](ROADMAP.md) links to Web v1 (M1–M5 plus M6 UI overhaul) and the independent Scribe v1 cycle (recording, transcription, fact extraction and review). Use real interview experience to prioritize further Web work before opening a possible Web v2 cycle.

## Ideas to explore

### Recording and interoperability

- Start/stop Scout.Scribe from the browser after explicit user initiation and consent, with a properly designed local control boundary.
- Transfer Scribe audio or transcripts into the selected Web interview without manual file import.
- Support Scribe on macOS or Linux, while keeping third-party audio/transcript tools usable.

### Interview review and knowledge

- Beyond [Scribe M3's planned local fact extraction and review](cycles/scout-scribe-v1/ROADMAP.md#milestone-3--fact-extraction-and-review), explore richer interview summaries, question/answer analysis, concerns and proposed actions alongside preserved sources.
- Explicitly promote selected supported facts/actions into structured records, without silent source overwrite or treating AI inferences as verified facts.
- Import and deduplicate existing question documents.
- Suggest interview questions with AI assistance.
- Retrieve relevant information from previous interviews with AI assistance.
- Personal interview knowledge chatbot.
- Suggest improvements to professional-experience answers.

### Company and vacancy research

- Germany-wide company discovery.
- Domain-based company categorization.
- Vacancy change detection and periodic checks.
- AI-assisted extraction of vacancy requirements.

### Application materials

- CV customization.
- Arbeitszeugnis analysis.
- Cover-letter drafting.

### Overview and reporting

- More sophisticated dashboard and reporting.

## Adding or refining an idea

A short note is enough. When useful, capture the problem, a real example, the desired outcome and open questions. Leave implementation choices and estimates open until the idea is understood.

Before moving an idea into the roadmap, identify the user need, scope, independent/manual fallback, privacy and source-preservation implications, and a concrete acceptance criterion. Create a milestone plan only when the work is actually prioritized. Existing constraints in the [technical specification](products/scout-web/TECHNICAL.md#5-important-constraints) continue to apply.
